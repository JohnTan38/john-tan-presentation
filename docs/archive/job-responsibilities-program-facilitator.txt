Assistant program facilitator
<company-overview>
365 Cancer Prevention Society is an IPC-approved non-profit in Singapore focused on cancer prevention through education, healthy lifestyle promotion, and patient support via counseling, wellness programs, and hospital visitations. Services are funded by public donations</company-overview>
Job Summary
Care for cancer patients and support Social Service Centre (SSC) activities by facilitating smooth operations, engaging clients, coordinating volunteers, and providing administrative assistance aligned with 365CPS’s mission.

<responsibilities>
Encourage and motivate clients to actively participate in SSC activities to support their health and social engagement
Reach out to new clients, including referrals and self-registered cases, to expand SSC support services
Assist in organizing and supervising SSC activities to ensure smooth and effective delivery
Coordinate volunteer involvement in SSC activities to optimize support and resource use
Engage with activity facilitators and participants to collect feedback for continuous improvement
Publicize SSC activities to raise awareness and increase community participation
Recommend and implement improvements to SSC activities based on feedback and observations
Follow up with absent clients to understand their needs and provide tailored support
Prepare and submit weekly reports on client activity and SSC operations to inform management decisions</responsibilities>
<Required competencies and certifications>
Minimum 2 years of experience, preferably in event organizing, to effectively manage SSC activities
Basic proficiency in Microsoft Office Excel, Word, and PowerPoint to support administrative tasks</Required competencies and certifications>
